No-Bake Peanut Butter Energy Bites

A quick, no-cook snack loaded with protein and oats, perfect for on-the-go fuel.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup mini chocolate chips
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions:

  1. Combine all ingredients in a medium mixing bowl.
  2. Stir thoroughly until the mixture becomes thick and slightly sticky.
  3. Roll the mixture into 3 cm bite-sized balls using your hands.
  4. Chill in the refrigerator for 30 minutes to set before eating.

High-Energy Trail Mix with Dark Chocolate

A portable, easily customized mix balancing salty nuts with sweet, antioxidant-rich dark chocolate.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup almonds
  • 1 cup walnuts
  • 1/2 cup pumpkin seeds
  • 1/2 cup dried cranberries
  • 1/2 cup dark chocolate chunks

Instructions:

  1. Gather all the ingredients into a large mixing bowl.
  2. Toss gently until the nuts, seeds, and chocolate are evenly distributed.
  3. Portion out into small airtight containers or resealable bags.
  4. Store in a cool, dry place for up to two weeks.

Crispy Roasted Sriracha Chickpeas

A savory, high-fiber, and spicy alternative to potato chips that crunches perfectly.

Ingredients:

  • 1 can chickpeas (rinsed and dried)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp sriracha sauce
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C and thoroughly pat the chickpeas dry with a paper towel.
  2. Toss the chickpeas with olive oil, sriracha, garlic powder, and salt.
  3. Spread evenly on a baking sheet.
  4. Roast for 25-30 minutes, shaking the pan halfway, until crunchy.

Greek Yogurt with Honey and Granola

A fresh, 5-minute prep snack offering a satisfying crunch and naturally sweet creaminess.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 cup crunchy oat granola
  • 1/4 cup fresh blueberries

Instructions:

  1. Spoon the Greek yogurt into a small serving bowl.
  2. Drizzle the honey evenly over the surface of the yogurt.
  3. Sprinkle the granola and fresh blueberries on top.
  4. Serve immediately to maintain the crunch of the granola.
