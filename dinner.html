Creamy Tuscan Garlic Chicken

A rich and popular comfort food featuring seared chicken bathed in a sun-dried tomato and spinach cream sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup sun-dried tomatoes
  • 1 cup fresh baby spinach
  • 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. Season and sear the chicken in a skillet over medium-high heat until cooked through, then set aside.
  2. In the same skillet, sauté garlic and sun-dried tomatoes for 1 minute.
  3. Stir in the heavy cream, spinach, and Parmesan, simmering until the spinach wilts.
  4. Return the chicken to the skillet, spooning the sauce over the top before serving.

Sheet Pan Teriyaki Salmon with Broccoli

A healthy, low-cleanup dinner pairing flaky salmon fillets with crisp, savory roasted broccoli.

Ingredients:

  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 3 tbsp teriyaki sauce
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Toss the broccoli with olive oil and arrange around the edges of the pan.
  3. Place the salmon in the center and brush generously with teriyaki sauce.
  4. Bake for 12-15 minutes, then garnish with sesame seeds.

Rustic Mushroom and Parmesan Risotto

A technique-focused vegetarian classic highlighting earthy mushrooms and a creamy, slow-cooked rice texture.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup Arborio rice
  • 4 cups warm vegetable broth
  • 1 cup sliced cremini mushrooms
  • 1/2 diced onion
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan

Instructions:

  1. Sauté the onions and mushrooms in butter until soft.
  2. Add the rice and toast for 1-2 minutes until translucent at the edges.
  3. Add the warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly until liquid is absorbed before adding more.
  4. Once rice is tender, remove from heat and stir in the Parmesan cheese.

Garlic Butter Steak Bites with Asparagus

A high-protein, quick-sear meal delivering tender, bite-sized steak coated in rich garlic butter.

Ingredients:

  • 400g sirloin steak cut into 3 cm cubes
  • 1 bunch trimmed asparagus
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves minced garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions:

  1. Season the steak cubes generously with salt and pepper.
  2. Sear the steak in a hot skillet for 2-3 minutes until browned, then remove.
  3. Add butter and garlic to the pan, tossing the asparagus until tender-crisp.
  4. Return the steak to the pan, toss everything in the garlic butter, and serve immediately.
