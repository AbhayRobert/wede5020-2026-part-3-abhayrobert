Grilled Chicken Caesar Salad Wrap

A familiar staple reinvented as a handheld meal, featuring crisp romaine and tender grilled chicken.

Ingredients:

  • 1 large flour tortilla
  • 1/2 cup sliced grilled chicken breast
  • 1 cup chopped romaine lettuce
  • 2 tbsp Caesar dressing
  • 1 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. Lay the tortilla flat on a clean surface.
  2. Toss the romaine lettuce and grilled chicken with Caesar dressing.
  3. Place the salad mixture in the center of the tortilla and sprinkle with Parmesan.
  4. Fold the sides inward and roll tightly into a wrap.

Quinoa and Roasted Chickpea Bowl

A vibrant, high-fiber vegan bowl loaded with plant-based protein and fresh vegetables.

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1/2 cup roasted spiced chickpeas
  • 1/2 cup halved cherry tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup diced cucumber
  • 2 tbsp tahini dressing

Instructions:

  1. Place the cooked quinoa as the base in a serving bowl.
  2. Arrange the roasted chickpeas, tomatoes, and cucumber over the quinoa.
  3. Drizzle the tahini dressing evenly over the top.
  4. Toss gently before eating

Caprese Panini with Fresh Basil Pesto

A quick-prep, vegetarian Italian sandwich bursting with melted mozzarella and fresh tomatoes.

Ingredients:

  • 2 slices ciabatta bread
  • 2 slices fresh mozzarella
  • 1 sliced Roma tomato
  • 2 tbsp fresh basil pesto
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions:

  1. Spread pesto evenly on the inside of both ciabatta slices.
  2. Layer the mozzarella and tomato slices between the bread.
  3. Brush the outside of the sandwich with olive oil.
  4. Grill in a panini press or skillet for 3-4 minutes per side until the cheese melts.

Spicy Peanut Soba Noodles

A 15-minute dairy-free noodle dish featuring a rich, savory, and slightly spicy peanut sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 120g soba noodles
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 chopped green onion

Instructions:

  1. Boil the soba noodles according to package directions, then drain and rinse with cold water.
  2. Whisk the peanut butter, soy sauce, sriracha, and sesame oil together until smooth.
  3. Toss the noodles in the sauce until fully coated.
  4. Garnish with chopped green onions before serving.
