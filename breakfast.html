Classic Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes

A comforting breakfast classic featuring golden-brown, thick pancakes with a soft, fluffy interior.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup melted butter

Instructions:

  1. Whisk dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
  2. Mix buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter in a separate jug.
  3. Fold wet ingredients into dry until just combined.
  4. Pour batter onto a hot greased griddle, flip when bubbles form, and cook until golden brown.

Smoked Salmon and Avocado Toast

A high-protein, 10-minute meal with creamy avocado and savory smoked salmon on crusty sourdough bread.

Ingredients:

  • 2 slices sourdough bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 120g smoked salmon
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Red pepper flakes

Instructions:

  1. Toast the sourdough slices until crisp.
  2. Mash the avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread the avocado evenly over the toast.
  4. Top with smoked salmon and a sprinkle of red pepper flakes.

Berry & Chia Seed Overnight Oats

A vegan-adaptable, make-ahead breakfast packed with fiber, fresh berries, and wholesome oats.

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup almond milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup mixed fresh berries

Instructions:

  1. In a jar, combine oats, almond milk, chia seeds, and maple syrup.
  2. Stir well to ensure chia seeds are evenly distributed.
  3. Top with fresh berries.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight before serving.

Spicy Shakshuka with Feta

A visually striking, one-pan Mediterranean dish of poached eggs in a robust, spiced tomato sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 diced onion
  • 1 diced red bell pepper
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese

Instructions:

  1. Sauté onion, bell pepper, and garlic in olive oil until soft.
  2. Stir in crushed tomatoes, cumin, and paprika, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Make 4 small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each.
  4. Cover and cook for 5-8 minutes until eggs are set, then top with crumbled feta.
