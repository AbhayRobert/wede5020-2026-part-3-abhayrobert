Chewy Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

A baking staple elevated by the nutty, caramelized depth of browned butter.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 cup unsalted butter (browned and cooled)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1.5 cups chocolate chips

Instructions:

  1. In a saucepan, melt butter until it foams and brown specks form, then let cool.
  2. Beat the browned butter with both sugars, then mix in the eggs.
  3. Stir in the flour until just combined, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Scoop dough onto a baking sheet and bake at 175°C for 10-12 minutes.

Campfire S'mores Skillet Dip

A fun, interactive dessert that brings the outdoor campfire experience straight to your kitchen.

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups chocolate chips
  • 1 bag large marshmallows
  • 1 box Tennis biscuits (for dipping)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 230°C.
  2. Pour the chocolate chips into the bottom of a 20 cm cast-iron skillet in an even layer.
  3. Arrange the marshmallows in a single layer completely covering the chocolate.
  4. Bake for 5-7 minutes until marshmallows are toasted brown, and serve with biscuits.

No-Bake Mixed Berry Cheesecake

A cool, vibrant presentation requiring zero oven time, featuring a fresh berry topping.

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups Tennis biscuit crumbs
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 450g cream cheese (softened)
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup mixed berry compote

Instructions:

  1. Mix Tennis biscuit crumbs and melted butter, pressing firmly into the bottom of a pie dish to form a crust.
  2. Beat the cream cheese, powdered sugar, and vanilla until completely smooth.
  3. Spread the cream cheese mixture evenly over the crust and chill for 4 hours.
  4. Top with the mixed berry compote right before serving.

Lemon Olive Oil Cake

A sophisticated, unique flavor profile yielding a wonderfully moist cake with a bright citrus finish.

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp lemon zest
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C and grease a 30 cm round cake pan.
  2. Whisk the eggs and sugar until pale, then gradually whisk in the olive oil, milk, lemon zest, and juice.
  3. Gently fold the flour into the wet ingredients until no lumps remain.
  4. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 35-40 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
